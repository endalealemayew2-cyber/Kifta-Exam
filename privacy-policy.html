Privacy Policy — Kifta: Exam

Package: com.kifta.exam · Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Contact: endalealemayew032@gmail.com

1. Who we are

Kifta: Exam ("the app", package name com.kifta.exam) is an offline-first exam preparation app for Ethiopian Grade 12, University Exit and TVET/COC candidates. It lets you practise past examination papers, read an explanation for each answer, and keep your own notes and bookmarks.

This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does with your information. In short: the app keeps your study data on your own device, and the only third parties involved are our rewarded-video ad networks — Google AdMob and Unity Ads — which serve the ads that keep the question bank free.

2. No account, no sign-up

You do not create an account, and the app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or any other personal detail. There is no login, no profile on our servers, and no way for us to identify you as an individual.

3. What is stored on your device

Everything you create while studying is saved locally, inside the app's private storage on your own device. It never leaves your device and is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

  • Your practice progress: which questions you have answered and how many free questions remain.
  • Your notes, bookmarks and saved questions.
  • Your examination history and scores.
  • Your settings: language, light or dark theme, and your ad-unlock balance.

You can erase all of this at any time from Settings → Reset. Uninstalling the app also removes it permanently. Because it lives only on your device, we cannot recover it for you if it is lost.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal data. We run no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no advertising server of our own. The app contains no tracking pixels and no social-media trackers.

5. Advertising and rewarded video

The app is free. To keep the question bank free, it shows rewarded video advertisements through two advertising networks: Google AdMob (part of Google Mobile Ads) and Unity Ads (provided by Unity Technologies). You only ever see an ad when you choose to watch one in order to unlock 40 more practice questions, or when you tap the unlock button — the app never plays an ad on its own.

AdMob is tried first. If AdMob cannot fill an ad (for example, when no ad is available for your region), the app automatically tries Unity Ads as a second source. If neither network can serve an ad, the app shows a short message and lets you try again, and it may unlock a few questions free so you are never blocked.

To select, deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Unity Ads SDK may collect and process certain information from your device, including your device's advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID), your IP address, general (coarse) location derived from the IP address, device type and operating system, and your interaction with the ad. Each network processes this information as an independent data controller under its own privacy policy.

The app itself never sees or stores this data. What the app learns from an ad is only whether the ad finished, so it can credit you the unlocked questions.

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy
  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity Ads data disclosure: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

6. Your consent choices

Before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires it — for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland — you are shown a consent form first, and you can decline personalised advertising. Your choice is passed on to both ad networks. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads or, where no ad can be served, the app simply tells you and lets you continue.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options, which reopens the Google consent form. You can also reset your Android Advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

7. Internet connection

The app needs an internet connection to download examination papers and to load ads. When it fetches a paper, our hosting provider may record ordinary web-server logs (such as the IP address and time of the request) for security and reliability. Those logs are not linked to you by us and are not used to build a profile of you.

Your study progress, notes and bookmarks are always available offline, because they are stored on your device.

8. Children's privacy

The app is intended for students who are preparing for examinations, generally aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you have a concern, please contact us and we will help.

9. Security

Your study data stays inside the app's private storage, protected by the Android operating system and, on modern devices, by the device's encryption. Because we never receive your data, there is no copy of it on our side that could be breached.

10. Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf — your data is already only on your device, and Settings → Reset removes it. For the advertising data that Google and Unity process, you can exercise your rights directly with them using the links in section 5, and you can withdraw ad consent at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date, and the new version will ship with the next app update. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact us

If you have any question about this policy or about privacy in the app, please email us at endalealemayew032@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.
